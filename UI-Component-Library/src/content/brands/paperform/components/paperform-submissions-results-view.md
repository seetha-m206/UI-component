> **RESOLVED 2026-09-23 (see §PF9 Update below) — `evidence_state` moved from `open_finding` to `source_reviewed`.** The original capture pass was blocked: a real submission failed because the account owner's email address was unverified, so the Submissions table could not be populated with real completed-submission rows (original note, kept for traceability: everything in the base sections below was confirmed from the empty-state UI and the full submissions app's other tabs — Partial Submissions, Products, Reports, Analytics — or explicitly marked unconfirmed). ## Location
- **Product:** Paperform
- **Screen(s) it appears on:** Share → Results (in-editor summary panel) and the full standalone app at `paperform.co/submissions/<slug>/` (tabs: Submissions · Partial Submissions · Products · Reports · Analytics). Tested on the published scratch form `xborqzxj` (all PF5–PF7 fields present: Q1–Q4, P1 Price, P2 Products, N1/N2 Number, C1 Calculation, one Custom PDF template).

## Structure
- **Share → Results (in-editor):** heading "N submissions of your form", a Search box, Delete…, Export…, and "Go to submissions →". Shows "No Submissions" at 0 rows.
- **Full app — Submissions tab:** a submissions-count tile + a 30-day daily line chart above a table (checkbox column, a configurable "Submission" title column, then one column per question, headers truncated). Toolbar: Search, Filter by date, a refresh icon, and a ⋮ menu (Display Settings · Export All · Delete All · View Deleted). Pagination reads "Page 1 of 1" at zero rows.
- **Display Settings modal:** controls which question becomes the row title (default: auto-select, falling back to submission ID), and **Column Visibility** toggles for Score, Customer, Total, and **PDFs** ("Show when available") plus Questions ("Show all") — confirming commerce/PDF columns exist in the schema even though they couldn't be populated this pass.
- **Full app — Partial Submissions tab:** a "Partial submissions are stored for 30 days" banner, a table (title = email answer + timestamp, expand icon, Started at, Last answered, one column per question), and a row-detail modal showing the partial ID (with copy), timestamps, and every answer as a copyable chip.
- **Full app — Products tab:** a stock-allocation ledger (Name, SKU, Total/Remaining/Allocated Stock, per-row Change, Reset All).
- **Full app — Reports tab:** a report builder — a field list, "Default Report", **Segments** ("All data" + "+" to add more, each segment built from a real rule-based query builder), "Add text", an "Insights ✨" AI feature (not tested), and per-question charts.
- **Full app — Analytics tab:** device filter, date range + a "compared to" period, headline metrics (Views/Viewers/Started/Submissions/Completion %), a by-day chart, and an explicitly-labeled **Export CSV**.

## Behavior & States
- **The list-level filter and the query-builder are two structurally separate surfaces** in this product — a genuinely notable architectural split: free-text search + date filtering lives on the raw Submissions list, while the real condition-based query builder (question/operator/value, And/Or, nested groups) lives only inside Reports → Segments, and drives aggregate reporting rather than filtering which raw rows are visible.
- **Partial submissions are captured even with "Automatic save and resume later" switched off** — confirmed directly (one partial exists from this pass despite that setting being off), consistent with the partial-submission streaming behavior already confirmed at the field level in [[paperform-yes-no-field]] and [[paperform-rating-field]].
- **The Calculation field evaluates on real submitted answers, not just sample values**, confirmed via the one partial's detail view: **C1 Total = 21.6**, matching `6 × 4 × 0.9` (quantity 6 × unit price 4 × the 10% discount from the [[paperform-calculation-field-ai-helper]] formula) — direct proof the calculation engine runs against live respondent data, not only the Calculation Editor's sample-value preview.
- **Confirmed UI oddity:** the partial's "Last answered" field reads "Q1" even though every field on the form was actually answered — a real, reproducible display bug, not a one-off.
- **Confirmed data-presentation choice:** Products appear in the partial's detail view by **SKU, not product name** (`dlf3j x 1`) — a real usability gap (SKUs are not human-friendly) distinct from any bug already found in [[paperform-payments-products-fields]].
- **Products/stock is unaffected by a partial submission** — the Products tab's stock ledger still showed Test Mug at 3/3/0 (Total/Remaining/Allocated) after the partial, confirming stock is only allocated on a genuine completed submission, not merely a partial answer capture.

## Rules & Validation
- **A hard submit-time guard existed: forms cannot accept submissions until the account owner's email is verified.** This blocked full completion of the original capture pass — confirmed via the exact HTTP 400 error text, not inferred. **RESOLVED in the PF9 follow-up** — the account owner verified their email, clearing the guard for all future submissions on this account.
- **Second-Pass Flags — all three resolved in the PF9 follow-up (see §PF9 Update):**
  1. ~~The Submissions table's actual column contents once real completed-submission rows exist~~ — **confirmed**: Total, Customer, Q1–C1 Total, PDFs (Score column absent when scoring is off).
  2. ~~Whether the submissions-export format is CSV, XLSX, or something else~~ — **confirmed CSV only**, delivered via a signed-URL `<a>` click, not a fetch/XHR call.
  3. ~~Whether payment status surfaces anywhere in the completed-submissions view~~ — **confirmed, and this is the pass's single most important finding**: it does surface, but as an unconditional "Total Charged"/"Total Paid" label with no unpaid/no-gateway indicator anywhere, even though no payment was actually taken.

## Technical Data
> OBSERVATION, directly captured via browser DOM/network inspection, Claude browser extension session, 2026-09-23.

- **Network — a distinct, paginated, client-fetched API call, unlike [[typeform-analytics-dashboard]]'s confirmed "no distinct fetch-results call" finding:** opening Share → Results fires `GET /api/v1/form/<formId>/submissions?page=1&id=<formId>&q=&orderby=created_at&order=desc&count=10` roughly 1s after mount (412 bytes for an empty result). **Confirmed fired twice with identical parameters** — a genuine duplicate-request bug on mount, not a capture artifact.
- **Submit payload shape (from the blocked attempt):** `{data:[{key,value}…], payment, captcha, score, partialSubmissionId, deviceId}`. With a $22 total and no payment gateway connected, `payment` was confirmed `null`, `captcha` was `null`, `score` was `false`.
- **The respondent side never makes this call** — the form definition is inlined into the page at load time, per [[respondent-runtime-guided-vs-standard]]'s own confirmed finding; this results-fetching behavior is specific to the owner-facing Results/Submissions surfaces.

## Best Observed Approach
- **RECOMMENDATION:** Zoho Forms' entries filter is the stronger list-level filtering implementation of the two directly comparable here — true datatype-aware query-building right on the list, vs. Paperform's search-and-date-only list with its real query builder relocated to Reports. Paperform's **partial-submissions-as-first-class-data** and **commerce/PDF-aware column schema** are both genuinely distinctive strengths with no counterpart recorded for either sibling. Set against that: the **confirmed duplicate submissions-fetch in the editor panel**, the **"SubmittedForm" analytics event firing on a rejected submit**, and — the most serious of the three, now confirmed with a real submission in PF9 — **a completed submission with no payment gateway connected is labeled "Total Charged"/"Total Paid" everywhere in the owner-facing UI, with no unpaid/no-gateway indicator anywhere**. That last point escalates [[paperform-payments-products-fields]]'s original "silent publish" finding from a configuration-time risk into a confirmed, live misrepresentation of real submission data to the form owner.

## Cross-Component Pattern Note
1. **Direct, load-bearing confirmation of [[paperform-calculation-field-ai-helper]]'s formula engine running correctly against real data** — first via the partial (`21.6`) and now reconfirmed on a genuine completed submission in PF9 (also `21.6`), matching the exact discount formula from that record — the strongest piece of end-to-end evidence in this entire Paperform research pass that the confirmed-correct Calculation Editor behavior genuinely holds on the live product, not just in the editor's own sample-value preview.
2. **A third confirmed anti-pattern/quality-gap category for this product**, alongside [[paperform-yes-no-field]]/[[paperform-rating-field]]'s accessibility defects and [[paperform-payments-products-fields]]'s business-logic safety gap: **basic engineering hygiene issues** (a duplicated network request — now confirmed editor-panel-specific in PF9 — a mislabeled "Last answered" field, an analytics event that fires on failure) — worth watching for as a recurring category in any future Paperform capture, not just accessibility or business-logic gaps.
3. **PF9 upgrades [[paperform-payments-products-fields]]'s "silent publish with no gateway" finding from a configuration-time risk to a confirmed, live data-integrity problem** — a real completed submission with `payment:null` (no gateway connected) is unconditionally shown as "Total Charged 22.00" in the owner-facing submission detail view and "Total Paid 22.00" in the editor's Results panel, with product stock genuinely allocated (Test Mug 3/3/0 → 3/2/1) despite no money changing hands. Both records should be read together going forward — cross-linked both directions.
4. **The submit-time email-verification guard, now resolved, is still worth remembering as a standing account-level fact for this specific research account** — any future prompt requiring a genuine completed submission on this account no longer needs to route around it, but the exact verification path is worth recording: it lives only in the avatar/account menu ("Verify your email address · Resend verification email"), not under Account Details.
5. **This Reports → Segments builder is one of three confirmed uses of the same shared condition-grammar pattern in this product** — [[paperform-question-visibility-logic]] (PF10) confirms the identical `[question][operator][value]`+And/Or+nested-group primitive also drives Question Visibility Logic and the Custom Pricing Rules builder ([[paperform-payments-products-fields]]), with this Segments builder and Visibility Logic both lacking the "then [action]" clause that only Pricing Rules has.
6. **See also [[google-forms-responses-view]]:** a fourth results-loading strategy across this library's four products — Google Forms fires distinct, on-demand, per-sub-view fetches (Summary/Question/Individual each hit a different endpoint), none of which reproduce this record's own confirmed duplicate-fetch bug.

## PF9 Update (2026-09-23) — Blocker Resolved, Real Completed Submission

> OBSERVATION, directly captured via browser DOM/network inspection, Claude browser extension session, 2026-09-23. Prerequisite completed first: the account owner verified their email address (via the avatar/account menu's "Verify your email address" control, not Account Details), clearing the guard documented above. The same representative data used in the original blocked attempt was resubmitted on the live form (Yes, test@example.com, "PF9 Tester", 4★, $10 deposit, 1×Test Mug, quantity 6, unit price 4 — "Submit — $22.00"). `POST .../submit` returned **200** (`ok:1`) and the respondent saw "Submission successful — Thanks! We have received your submission."; `PUT …/event` (SubmittedForm) followed, this time legitimately.

**Populated Submissions row, full app (`/submissions/<slug>/`):** column order is ☐ · Submission (title) · **Total** · **Customer** · Q1…C1 Total · **PDFs**. Confirmed values: Total `22.00`, Customer `-` (no gateway means no customer object), **Score column absent entirely** (not blank — hidden, since scoring is off and "Show when available" suppresses it), Q-columns matching the submitted answers, Products still shown by SKU (`dlf3j x 1`, the same usability gap already noted for the partial), C1 Total `21.6`. **PDFs is a ⋮ button**, not a link, opening a menu: "Download PDF summary" and "Submission Results" (the PF7 custom PDF, named). The row's own ⋮ menu additionally offers Custom PDFs…, Download Word summary, Rerun integration actions (disabled), Resend webhooks (disabled), Download all files (disabled), Copy link, Delete.

**The editor's own Share → Results panel uses different column labels for the identical underlying data** — "Total Paid" instead of "Total," "Customer ID" instead of "Customer" — a confirmed, real label inconsistency between the two owner-facing surfaces for the same submission.

**Row detail view — a full page, not a modal, structurally different from the Partial Submissions detail:** route `/submissions/<slug>/inbox?selected_submission_id=<id>`, an inbox-style layout with a left list ("← Back to Submissions") and prev/next navigation. New versus the partial's popup: a metadata card (Submission ID with copy, Submitted At, **IP Address, Device, Platform, Browser**) and a **"Total Charged: 22.00"** card, plus a "Search question titles…" box. Unlike the partial, it has no "Started at/Last answered" pair. Answer chips and SKU-only product display carry over unchanged from the partial's own pattern.

**Export — format and delivery mechanism now confirmed, explaining a genuine capture gap from the original pass:** clicking Export → All shows a toast ("Exporting submissions...") but **fires no fetch/XHR at all** — the client instead builds a signed URL and triggers it via a programmatic `<a>.click()`: `GET /api/v1/form/<id>/submissions/export-csv/download?expires=…&signature=…`, response `200`, `Content-Type: text/csv`. This is *why* the original PF8 pass's network monitoring never saw an export request even if one had been clicked — the mechanism itself bypasses ordinary fetch/XHR instrumentation. **The format is CSV only** — no XLSX or other option was ever offered anywhere in this path. The CSV's columns are notably broader than the on-screen table: Submitted At, one column per question, **per-SKU product-quantity columns**, a **signed S3 URL to the rendered PF7 PDF**, Total Amount, Refunded Amount, Customer ID, **respondent IP address**, submission ID, UTM Source/Medium/Campaign/Term/Content, and Device Type. **This export is genuinely sensitive** (respondent IP address, a time-limited but real signed PDF download link) — worth flagging for any future prompt or preview that touches this data, not just a "here's the format" footnote.

**Payment status — the single most important finding of this update, confirmed exactly as flagged as a risk in [[paperform-payments-products-fields]]:** with no payment gateway connected and the submit payload's own `payment` field `null`, the real completed submission is nonetheless labeled, unconditionally and with no caveat anywhere: "Total 22.00" (list), "Total Charged 22.00" (detail view), "Total Paid 22.00" (editor Results panel), "Total Amount 22" (CSV export). **There is no "unpaid," "no gateway," or equivalent warning state anywhere in the owner-facing UI.** The form owner has no way to tell, from any of these four surfaces, that no money actually changed hands.

**Network, refined:** the full `/submissions/<slug>/` app's own list uses `GET .../submissions/table` (fired once) — a genuinely different endpoint from the editor's Share → Results panel's `GET .../submissions` (still fired **twice** on mount, reproducing the original duplicate-request bug now with real data present) — confirming the duplicate-fetch bug is specific to the editor panel, not the product's results-loading strategy as a whole. Opening a row's detail view fires its own distinct request, `GET .../submissions/paginate?after_id&before_id&include_submission_id`, which also prefetches the adjacent rows for prev/next navigation.

**Stock, confirmed:** after this one real completed submission, the Products tab's Test Mug ledger moved from 3 Total/3 Remaining/0 Allocated to **3 Total/2 Remaining/1 Allocated** — a real, concrete instance of stock being committed against a submission with no payment actually taken, directly connecting this finding to [[paperform-payments-products-fields]]'s own confirmed safety gap. A per-row "Reset" control can restore the allocation.

**Residual test data (left in place, not cleaned up, consistent with this project's practice of leaving genuine non-sensitive test artifacts):** the scratch form now holds 1 completed submission (`test@example.com` / "PF9 Tester", total 22.00) and 1 partial submission, with 1 unit of Test Mug stock allocated (reversible via Products → Reset). The CSV export was triggered 3 times during mechanism discovery, leaving up to 3 copies in the browser's own downloads folder — not this repository.

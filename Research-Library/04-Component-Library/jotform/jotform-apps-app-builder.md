## Location

Reached two ways, both tested live: (1) Products ▾ top-nav dropdown → "Jotform Apps" → a dedicated marketing landing page at `jotform.com/products/apps/` ("Jotform | Apps", tagline "Create Apps with App Maker"); (2) the dashboard's "+ CREATE" → "How would you like to start?" picker → "App" card ("Make downloadable mobile apps with no coding"), a sibling option alongside Form, E-sign, Workflow, AI Agent, Table, Report, Board, Website Widget. FACT

## Structure

**1. What it actually is, confirmed live: an app-bundling/wrapper product, not a forms or logic product in its own right.** FACT The landing page's own description: "Jotform's free app maker lets you create fully customized apps with forms, widgets, products, and your branding or generate instantly with AI. Apps can be downloaded to any smartphone or computer." Opening the builder confirms this: the BUILD tab is a page/block composer (a scrollable mobile-app canvas with a hero image, heading, buttons, and member-card-style content blocks), not a field-entry form. The "+" add-element picker is headed "App Elements" with its own BASIC-tab vocabulary distinct from Form Builder's field palette: Form, Heading, Rich Text, Text, List, Card, Sign Document, Link, Image Gallery, Button, Social Follow, Testimonial, Accordion, Tabs, Shortcuts, Banner, and more below the fold (not fully enumerated). Two of these block types — Form and Sign Document — directly embed a Jotform form or a Jotform Sign document inside an app page; there is a separate WIDGETS tab (not opened this pass). FACT

**2. Starting point: a richer-than-3-way pattern — AI generation (front-and-center and tested end-to-end), Start from scratch, Use template, plus a bonus storefront variant.** FACT — confirmed via the "+ CREATE → App" chooser. The chooser screen ("Describe your app — Bring your app idea to life without coding") puts an AI-prompt box first and most prominently (placeholder: "Create an online learning platform with course creation and student progress tracking."; Upload/Add Link buttons; 4 example-prompt chips: Membership Manager, Feedback Analyzer, Inventory Tracker, Lease Organizer), then four cards below: Start from scratch ("A blank state is all you need"), Use template ("Choose from 3.5K+ premade apps"), and Build store ("Collect orders through your online store" — a fourth, commerce-specific variant not seen in Sign Builder's 3-way pattern). This matches/exceeds the 3-way pattern already confirmed for Jotform Sign in [[jotform-sign-builder]] (Upload/Template/AI), substituting "Upload a document" for "Start from scratch" plus adding a dedicated storefront option. FACT **Addendum, 2026-10-06**: once at least one app already exists on the account, a fifth card appears — "Clone existing app" ("Create a copy of one of your existing apps") — not present on the very first visit to this chooser, confirming the option set itself is conditioned on account state, consistent with the mode-switcher conditionality documented in Behavior & States below. FACT

"Start from scratch" was completed end-to-end this session (2026-10-06), resolving the original pass's open flag. It opens directly into the BUILD tab on a genuinely blank app — a placeholder "App Title" hero and a single "Drag your first element here from left" drop zone, no pre-built content, no DATA tab, and a 3-tab (BUILD/SETTINGS/PUBLISH) mode bar rather than the AI-path's 4-tab one. FACT

The AI path was tested end-to-end live: typed "Create a coffee club loyalty app with a signup form and a rewards tracker" into the landing-page prompt box and clicked Send. This navigated to a dedicated route, `jotform.com/app/build/ai-creation/`, where a named, branded conversational AI agent — "Podo, an AI Agent" (same assistant persona seen cross-product in this library; see Behavior & States below) — showed a "Thinking..." state for roughly 8–12 seconds, then landed directly in the BUILD tab with a fully-built, multi-element app already assembled: a coffee-shop hero photo, "Coffee Club" heading, a "Join Coffee Club" button, and two seeded loyalty-member card rows (sample data: "Jamie Lee — One stamp away, Stamps: 7" and "Alex Morgan"). The AI also auto-provisioned a matching Jotform Tables table ("Coffee Club Signup," pre-seeded with the same two sample rows: Full Name, Email, Favorite Coffee, Loyalty Stamps columns) and, per the Workflows record, a matching Jotform Workflow and auto-named Workflow Board were separately generated from the same account context during this pass (see Technical Data). FACT

## Behavior & States

**3. Mode-switcher reuse: present, and its richness is CONDITIONAL on whether the app has a Form block attached — not a fixed property of the Apps product.** FACT — revised 2026-10-06 with a materially more precise finding than the original 2026-10-05 pass. This was directly tested two ways in the same session, isolating the variable:

- On the AI-generated "Coffee Club" app (2026-10-05): the "App Builder ▾" dropdown showed only an "App Builder" tile (itself, highlighted active) and "Go Back to My Workspace" — no other product tiles at all, despite that app having a DATA tab backed by an auto-created Tables table.
- On a "Start from scratch" blank app (2026-10-06): immediately after creation (no elements added), the mode bar showed only BUILD / SETTINGS / PUBLISH (no DATA tab at all). After adding a "Form" block from the App Elements panel and attaching the account's existing "Coffee Club Signup" form, the DATA tab appeared, and — separately and more strikingly — the mode-switcher dropdown expanded to the full shared grid: View Form, Form Builder, PDF Editor, Tables, Inbox, Form Analytics, Report Builder, Workflow Builder, App Builder (active), Boards, Go Back to My Workspace (11 tiles — missing only AI Agent Builder).

This resolves an inconsistency with [[jotform-ai-agents]], which originally characterized "Apps' ... reduced-but-still-recognizable switcher" as having "retained Form Builder/Tables/Inbox tiles" — that description is accurate only for a Form-attached app (as directly confirmed here), not for Apps in general; the originally-tested AI-generated app had no such tiles at all. [[jotform-ai-agents]] has been updated with a correction note reflecting this.

The precise trigger for the full-switcher expansion is still not fully isolated: it could be "any Form block present," "any object with Form-Builder-compatible training wheels attached," or something else — the AI-generated app had a Tables backing store but apparently no literal "Form" block element placed on its canvas (its content was Hero/Heading/Button/Card blocks bound to table data, not an embedded Form), which is the leading hypothesis for why it got the reduced switcher despite having real account data behind it. INFERENCE, flagged for further isolation — see Second-Pass Flags. What is now directly confirmed as FACT, not inference, is that the switcher's content is not static per-product — the same App Builder shell shows a 2-tile or an 11-tile switcher depending on the app's own content.

**4. Coupling to existing products: tightly coupled to both Forms and Tables, confirmed via two independent, concrete findings — and the DATA tab itself is conditional, not a fixed tab.** FACT First, the "Form" and "Sign Document" block types in the App Elements palette let an app page embed a real Jotform form or Sign document directly — not a simplified/app-specific re-implementation of field collection, but the literal existing products surfaced as insertable blocks, confirmed live by picking an actual existing account form ("Coffee Club Signup") from a real "Select Forms" picker (search box, submission counts, last-modified dates — the same picker UX as elsewhere in the account). Second, and more concretely, the DATA tab is literally a Jotform Tables grid rendered inline inside the App Builder shell — but, confirmed 2026-10-06, this tab only appears once a data-bearing block (a Form) is actually attached; a brand-new blank app shows BUILD/SETTINGS/PUBLISH only, no DATA tab. Once "Coffee Club Signup" was added as a Form block, the DATA tab appeared showing that form's real, live Tables data — all 3 actual submissions on the account at the time (including a "Test User" test row added in an unrelated JF2 pass), confirming this is the literal same underlying table, not a snapshot or copy. This is a direct structural parallel to the Sign→Tables coupling already confirmed in [[jotform-sign-builder]] ("Clone of {Document Title}"), but here the coupling is even more visible — once present, Tables isn't just auto-provisioned alongside the app, it's embedded as one of the app's own primary tabs. FACT

**5. Pricing/plan gating: no lock icons or paywall banners encountered anywhere in Apps' BUILD/DATA/SETTINGS/PUBLISH flow on this account.** FACT, with an important account-level caveat. This account is on a free plan limited to 5 forms (confirmed separately via a "Get more forms - for free" referral-style upgrade prompt encountered mid-session, cross-checked against the All Forms list showing exactly 5 forms present). Despite that cap, every screen tested in Apps — the AI-generation flow, the BUILD canvas, the DATA tab, SETTINGS (including AI CHATBOT), and PUBLISH (QUICK SHARE, EMBED, APP USERS, PUSH NOTIFICATIONS) — rendered fully, with no visible lock icon, "Upgrade to unlock," or blurred-preview pattern. This does not confirm Apps is unlimited on the free plan (a form-count-style cap could exist but simply not have been hit with only one app created this pass), but no gating was observed. FACT/OBSERVATION — flagged for a second pass.

**Competitor equivalent: none found in this library.** Per the brief's own framing, no sibling competitor record in this Research-Library currently documents an app-bundling/wrapper product (a tool that packages existing forms/widgets/data into a branded, downloadable native-feeling mobile or desktop app). This is confirmed, not merely assumed — Competitor Comparisons is intentionally left empty, and this should be treated as a genuine gap/opportunity flag for the library rather than an oversight in this file.

## Rules & Validation

- The AI-generation flow produced a fully populated, immediately-publishable app with no intermediate confirmation or editing step required before landing in BUILD — the generated result is live and editable immediately, not a locked preview.
- "Add to Home Screen Modal" and "Search Bar" toggles in APP SETTINGS both default to on.
- App Status defaults to ENABLED ("Your app is currently available to anyone with the link") immediately upon creation — no explicit "go live" step was required before the PUBLISH tab's Quick Share link became functional.

## Technical Data

- URL pattern for AI-generation: `jotform.com/app/build/ai-creation/`, which resolves (after generation completes) to `jotform.com/app/build/{appID}/{tab}` — e.g., `.../262771841024455/publish`.
- URL pattern for a Boards instance auto-created from this same account context: `jotform.com/boards/{boardID}` (see [[jotform-boards]] for the finding that a board opened via a different product's mode-switcher can be auto-scoped/pre-named rather than generic).
- The branded AI agent persona "Podo" appeared identically (same name, same cat-mascot illustration style) in both the App Builder's AI-creation flow and, separately, in the Workflow Builder's chat-assistant panel (see [[jotform-workflows-workflow-builder]]) — confirming this is a cross-product, platform-level AI-assistant brand, not an App-Builder-specific feature. **Confirmed, 2026-10-06**: Podo is also present as a persistent, collapsed-by-default chat panel inside the BUILD tab of a from-scratch app — a small floating mascot-icon bubble in the canvas's bottom-left corner that, when clicked, opens a labeled "Podo — App Copilot" panel with a fresh greeting ("Welcome to App Copilot! I'm here to help you build apps quickly."), four app-specific suggestion chips (Loyalty Tracker, Inventory Manager, Feedback Hub, Event Scheduler), a "Build with Podo..." text input, a "Draw & Edit" button (sketch-to-app generation, not tested further), and a "Talk" voice-input button. This directly confirms Podo is not limited to the one-shot AI-creation flow — it is a standing, always-available in-builder assistant on every app regardless of how it was started, matching how it behaves in Workflows. FACT
- The App Elements palette vocabulary (Form, Heading, Rich Text, Text, List, Card, Sign Document, Link, Image Gallery, Button, Social Follow, Testimonial, Accordion, Tabs, Shortcuts, Banner) is a page/block-composer vocabulary, structurally closer to a website-builder's block list than to Form Builder's field-type palette — supporting the "wrapper/composer product" characterization in Structure finding #1.

## Best Observed Approach

The AI-generation path producing a fully assembled, multi-surface result in one step is the standout finding here: a single natural-language prompt produced not just a styled app UI, but a matching backing Tables store pre-seeded with plausible sample data, in well under 30 seconds, with zero additional configuration required before the app was publishable. RECOMMENDATION — this is a notably more complete one-shot result than the Smart PDF Forms AI-conversion pipeline documented in [[jotform-smart-pdf-forms]] (which required a source document) or the Sign Builder's AI path (not completed end-to-end in that pass), worth flagging as JotForm's strongest "AI-generation" demonstration observed in this library so far.

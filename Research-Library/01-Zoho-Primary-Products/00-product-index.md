## How this index is used

1. Each row's `Record` file is created from `../00-Framework/templates/product-template.md`.
2. Once a Zoho product record identifies its real competitors (Section 4 of the template), those competitors get their own records under `../02-Competitor-Products/<category>/`.
3. A `_benchmark.md` (from `competitor-comparison-template.md`) rolls up the category once enough competitor records exist.
4. **Row 2 (Zoho Social) is the fully worked example** — see `zoho-social.md` and `../02-Competitor-Products/social-media-management/` for the pattern to replicate across the rest of this table.

## Expansion beyond Zoho

Per the research brief, this library is **not limited to Zoho**. Once the Zoho primary products and their direct competitors are documented, the same category folders under `02-Competitor-Products/` become the home for researching other major companies' products in those same categories (e.g., a full HubSpot or Salesforce record lives under `crm-sales/`, a full Asana or Monday.com record lives under `project-work-management/`), so the library reads as one unified competitive map per category rather than "Zoho vs. everyone else."

## 2. Market & Business
- **Pricing (FACT, vendor-model as described by aggregator sources, retrieved 2026-09-11 — not independently fetched from tawk.to's own pricing page this pass):** Core platform is free forever, with no cap on number of agents, chat volume, or connected sites/properties; includes live chat, email ticketing, a hosted knowledge base, basic CRM, chat pages, triggers, and reporting at no cost, plus 24/7 live support.
- **Paid add-ons (FACT, aggregator-sourced):** "AI Assist" starting around $29/month (adds 24/7 automated chat, agent suggestions, multilingual chat, and knowledge-base-grounded answers); Video + Voice + Screensharing around $29/month on annual billing; "Hired Agents" (tawk.to's own staffed live-chat agents) starting around $1/hour. **All figures third-party sourced via WebSearch aggregation — flagged for re-verification against the official tawk.to pricing page.**
- **Free plan/trial:** No trial needed — the core product itself is permanently free with no seat/agent cap, which is the product's primary market differentiator in this category (contrast with LiveChat's no-free-tier, 30-day-trial model — see `livechat.md`).
- **Market positioning (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + INFERENCE):** Positioned explicitly as the free/low-cost disruptor in the live-chat category — reviewers and aggregators (chatim.app, retrieved 2026-09-11) describe it as "the most-installed free Zoho SalesIQ alternative."
- **Key differentiators claimed by vendor/reviewers (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Zero-cost unlimited-agent model; fast (reported ~2-minute) setup; simplicity, especially for beginners/small businesses; optional paid staffed-agent service (a differentiator not offered by most competitors in this category).

## 15. What We Should Learn
- **RECOMMENDATION — adopt (for a free/freemium go-to-market, not necessarily for Zoho SalesIQ itself):** tawk.to's free-forever, unlimited-agent core model is the most consistently and enthusiastically praised aspect across the review sources gathered here ("best chat application on the market... and it's FREE") — a strong signal that removing seat-count and per-agent cost friction entirely, rather than just offering a limited free tier (as Zoho SalesIQ's 3-operator Free plan does — see `../../01-Zoho-Primary-Products/zoho-salesiq.md` Section 2), is a powerful acquisition lever for the SMB/startup segment specifically (derived from Section 5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK).
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate before adopting:** tawk.to monetizes via paid add-ons layered on top of a free core (AI Assist, video/voice/screensharing, Hired Agents) rather than per-seat tiers — this "free core + paid capability add-ons" model is structurally different from every other product researched in this category so far (Zoho SalesIQ, LiveChat, Drift all gate core capacity/seats) and is worth a dedicated comparison of long-term monetization durability (INFERENCE from Section 2 pricing-model comparison across this record and the others).
- **RECOMMENDATION — avoid:** The recurring complaint that chats can only be received while actively logged into the dashboard (no passive/offline notification path mentioned) is a specific, avoidable UX gap relative to the general expectation of push-style engagement in this category (derived from Section 5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK).

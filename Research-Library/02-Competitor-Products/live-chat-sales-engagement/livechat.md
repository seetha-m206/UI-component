## 1. Identity
- **Company (FACT):** LiveChat is the flagship product of Text S.A. (Warsaw Stock Exchange: TXT), a Polish public company headquartered in Wrocław, Poland; formerly named "LiveChat Software S.A." before a 2023 corporate rebrand to "Text" to reflect a broadened AI/e-commerce focus (en.wikipedia.org — "Text (company)", retrieved 2026-09-11). **Founded (FACT):** 2002, by Mariusz Ciepły, Maciej Jarzębowski, and Jakub Sitarz (per WebSearch aggregation of company-history sources, retrieved 2026-09-11) — original public source not independently re-fetched in this pass, flagged for re-verification.
- **Category:** Live Chat & Sales Engagement — direct competitor to Zoho SalesIQ; also sold as customer-service/helpdesk software (the company's product suite additionally includes "ChatBot" and "HelpDesk" as separate products, per Wikipedia summary, retrieved 2026-09-11), so it overlaps somewhat with the Customer Support/Helpdesk category as well.
- **Problem solved (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK / vendor positioning, retrieved 2026-09-11):** Real-time website live chat and customer communication across chat, email, and social channels, aimed at sales and support teams who need a dedicated, mature, and highly-rated live-chat tool rather than a bundled module inside a larger CRM/support suite.
- **Target users / industries (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, capterra.com, retrieved 2026-09-11):** Capterra reviewer mix skews small business (72% of reviewers), with Computer Software the largest single sector at 7%; also used by e-commerce stores, B2B SaaS companies, customer-support departments, sales teams, and some enterprises.
- **Segment:** SMB-to-mid-market primarily, per reviewer mix (see above), with tiered per-seat pricing scaling toward larger teams (Section 2).
- **Platforms:** Web widget confirmed; TODO — mobile app existence/parity not independently verified this pass.
- **Ecosystem / sister products it integrates with:** Sister products under the same parent company (Text S.A.) include ChatBot and HelpDesk (FACT, per company-overview source, retrieved 2026-09-11); depth of integration between LiveChat and these sister products NOT OBSERVED this pass.

- **Pricing (FACT, aggregator-sourced from Capterra pricing summary, retrieved 2026-09-11 — not independently fetched from livechat.com's own pricing page this pass, flagged for re-verification):** Starter ~$25/user/month (aggregator variance noted: a separate WebSearch summary cited a $19/month entry point in a different comparison context — **discrepancy not resolved in this pass, needs a direct fetch of livechat.com/pricing to reconcile**), Team ~$59/user/month, Business ~$89/user/month, and a custom-quote Enterprise tier. - **Market positioning (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + INFERENCE):** Positioned as a mature, design-polished, dedicated live-chat incumbent — one of the longest-running products in the category (founded 2002) — competing on UX quality and support responsiveness rather than being bundled "free" or as part of a larger CRM suite.
- **Key differentiators claimed by vendor/reviewers (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Intuitive interface; canned responses and tagging features that streamline support; consistently highly-rated support-team responsiveness; smooth onboarding.

## 5. Customer Reviews
- **G2 rating (FACT):** Approximately 4.5/5, with 69% of reviewers giving a full 5-star rating (g2.com/products/livechat/reviews, retrieved 2026-09-11 via WebSearch aggregation — direct page not independently fetched this pass). A related/distinct listing "LiveChat Service" also appears on G2 (g2.com/products/livechat-service/reviews) — **not confirmed whether this is the same product/SKU or a separate one; flagged similarly to the Zendesk/Intercom G2 family-count caveat used elsewhere in this library.**
- **Capterra rating (FACT):** 4.7/5 from approximately 1,649 reviews (capterra.com/p/62194/LiveChat/, retrieved 2026-09-11).
- **Other aggregator scores cited (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, third-party sourced, not independently re-verified):** ~4.6/5 on Capterra (a slightly different figure from a separate WebSearch pass — treat the 4.7/1,649 Capterra figure above as the primary number since it cites a specific review count) and ~7.3/10 on TrustRadius.
- **Liked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, aggregated from Capterra/G2 review-theme summaries, retrieved 2026-09-11):** Intuitive, user-friendly interface for managing multiple conversations; canned-response and tagging features that streamline support workflows; consistently well-rated customer-support responsiveness/helpfulness from the LiveChat team itself; smooth onboarding; good integrations and multi-channel engagement; reviewers describe pricing as generally "fair value" and easy to justify against the feature set, despite per-seat cost concerns (see Disliked below).
- **Disliked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, same sources):** Pricing described as having "increased dramatically over the last few years," making per-seat scaling to multiple agents/teams expensive; a specific structural complaint that ticketing, which was previously bundled into the live-chat plan, was moved into a separate paid product (HelpDesk) — reviewers characterize this as "nickel and diming long-term loyal users"; customization described as complex without technical expertise; the chatbot add-on described by some as expensive and by others as "frustrating to use"; at least one reviewer reported persistent reliability problems ("paid for this live chat service for years and it has consistently failed to work properly") — a single data point, not a broad reliability pattern established in this pass.
- **Recurring complaints:** Rising per-seat pricing over time; unbundling of previously-included features (ticketing) into separately-priced products; chatbot cost/usability; customization complexity for non-technical users.
- **Recurring praise:** UI/UX quality and ease of managing multi-conversation workflows; canned responses/tagging; support-team responsiveness; onboarding smoothness.

## 15. What We Should Learn
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate before adopting:** LiveChat's move to unbundle ticketing out of the core live-chat plan into a separately-sold product (HelpDesk) is the single most consistently criticized structural change across the review sources gathered here — a clear signal that removing previously-bundled value from an existing plan generates disproportionate customer resentment even if the new pricing is "fair" in isolation (derived from Section 5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK).
- **RECOMMENDATION — adopt:** LiveChat's consistently well-rated onboarding and support-team responsiveness stand out relative to other products in this category researched so far (Zoho SalesIQ's Capterra Customer Service sub-score is lower, 4.1/5) — worth investigating what specifically LiveChat does in its support/onboarding motion that produces this gap (derived from cross-record comparison, Section 5 here vs. `../../01-Zoho-Primary-Products/zoho-salesiq.md` Section 5).
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate further:** The G2/Capterra rating discrepancy pattern (multiple slightly different numbers depending on source/page) recurs across nearly every product in this library — reinforces the standing RECOMMENDATION (see `zoho-desk.md`, `intercom.md`) to always do a direct primary-source fetch before quoting a rating externally, rather than relying on aggregator summaries.

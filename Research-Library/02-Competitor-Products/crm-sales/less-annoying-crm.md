# Less Annoying CRM — Competitor Research Record

> Stub created as part of the Zoho Bigin research pass (2026-09-11) — Less Annoying CRM (LACRM) was selected as the best-substantiated new micro-business/simple-CRM competitor to document alongside Zoho Bigin (`../../01-Zoho-Primary-Products/zoho-bigin.md`), since both target very small businesses/solo operators with an intentionally simple, low-cost product rather than competing with full-featured CRMs like Zoho CRM, Salesforce, or HubSpot. Sections 1–5 (identity, pricing, reviews) are researched from public sources; Sections 6–13 (UI/UX, technical, AI, mobile, security) are `NOT OBSERVED` — no live product exploration was performed in this pass.

## 1. Identity
- **Company (FACT):** Less Annoying CRM, LLC — bootstrapped, family/founder-owned (not venture-backed), headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri; founded 2009 by two brothers (Tyler King, CEO, and a co-founder), initially self-funded while both founders held other jobs (WebSearch summary aggregating PitchBook/Latka/Crunchbase/company-about-page snippets, retrieved 2026-09-11).
- **Category:** CRM & Sales.
- **Problem solved (FACT, vendor-stated):** Positioned as a "middle ground between spreadsheets and complex enterprise CRM solutions" — full contact/relationship history (notes, follow-ups, meetings, emails), task reminders, and calendar sync in one simple tool. (Source: lessannoyingcrm.com homepage, WebFetch, retrieved 2026-09-11.)
- **Target users / industries (FACT, vendor-stated):** Small businesses and solo entrepreneurs wanting an intuitive CRM "without unnecessary complexity or lengthy learning curves." (Source: lessannoyingcrm.com, retrieved 2026-09-11.)
- **Segment:** Micro-business / solo operator — same segment Zoho Bigin targets (INFERENCE, comparing vendor-stated positioning to `zoho-bigin.md` Section 1).
- **Platforms:** Web-based; mobile app existence not confirmed in the content fetched in this pass — TODO.
- **Ecosystem / sister products:** None found — appears to be a single-product company, unlike Zoho (which sells Bigin alongside Zoho CRM and the wider Zoho One suite). TODO to confirm no companion products exist.
- **Company scale (FACT, WebSearch summary of PitchBook/Latka, retrieved 2026-09-11):** ~23–24 employees; serves "10,000+ small businesses" per vendor homepage copy; estimated ARR ~$5.4M per Latka (2024 figure — third-party estimate, unverified against any official disclosure, and the company is private/bootstrapped so no independent financial confirmation exists).

## 5. Customer Reviews
- **G2 (FACT, third-party/WebSearch sourced — no direct page fetch performed in this pass):** ~4.8/5 rating per WebSearch summary describing it as "the highest-rated CRM on both G2 and Capterra" (retrieved 2026-09-11) — review count for G2 specifically not captured; **UNVERIFIED, needs direct page fetch.**
- **Capterra (FACT — confirmed via direct page fetch of capterra.com/p/118921/Less-Annoying-CRM/reviews/, retrieved 2026-09-11):** 4.8/5 rating, 657 verified reviews. Note: a separate WebSearch summary cited "645 verified reviews (629 positive/11 neutral/5 negative)" from a different retrieval moment — the direct-fetch figure (657) is treated as most current/authoritative for this pass, discrepancy flagged per evidence-guidelines.md.
- **Liked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra direct fetch, retrieved 2026-09-11):**
  - Ease of use / simplicity — most consistently cited strength; one reviewer paraphrase: can "see where every search stands...without digging through layers of unnecessary features."
  - Affordability — explicitly compared favorably to Salesforce and HubSpot.
  - Customer support — described as responsive and human ("gotten help from an actual person who knew what they were talking about" — reviewer paraphrase); support quality is a more prominently and specifically praised theme here than in the Bigin or Pipedrive review sets gathered so far.
  - Customization without added complexity — custom fields, tags, and groups available without a steep technical learning curve.
- **Disliked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, same source):**
  - Limited advanced features — no workflow/project-tracking capability, no bulk email functionality cited as gaps.
  - Reporting limitations — users want more customizable reporting/pivoting without needing to export to CSV.
  - Missing automation — recurring-task and broader automation features occasionally requested.
- **Recurring complaints:** Feature ceiling for advanced/automation needs; reporting limited to CSV export workarounds (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, as above).
- **Recurring praise:** Simplicity; affordability; support quality; light-touch customization (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, as above).
- **Requested features:** Bulk email, workflow/project tracking, recurring-task automation, richer in-app reporting (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra, retrieved 2026-09-11).
- **Why customers switch away / choose it:** INFERENCE only — plausibly chosen over Salesforce/HubSpot/Zoho CRM for simplicity, price transparency, and support quality; plausibly switched away from once a team needs workflow automation, bulk email, or deeper reporting — not yet backed by direct switch-reason quotes.

## 15. What We Should Learn
- **RECOMMENDATION — adopt:** A single, fully-featured flat-rate pricing tier (no add-on gating, no per-tier feature withholding) is strongly associated with the highest customer-satisfaction ratings gathered anywhere in this category so far (~4.8/5 vs. 4.1–4.7/5 for tiered competitors) — worth weighing directly against Bigin's/Pipedrive's tiered-ladder approach (derived from Section 2 FACT + Section 5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, and cross-referenced against `zoho-bigin.md` and `pipedrive.md` pricing sections).
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate before adopting:** LACRM's support-quality praise is unusually specific and prominent compared to other CRM review sets gathered in this category — worth understanding what "responsive, human, US-based support" looks like operationally before assuming it's replicable at larger scale (derived from Section 5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK).
- **RECOMMENDATION — avoid:** Leaving reporting so basic that users resort to CSV export as a workaround — the same category-wide "shallow reporting" complaint recurs here as it does for Zoho Bigin (derived from Section 5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, cross-referenced with `zoho-bigin.md` Section 5).

## 5. Customer Reviews
- **G2 rating:** Sources vary noticeably even within this pass's independent search — a seller/aggregate page reports **4.1/5 (~7,221–7,383 reviews)**; a separate product-listing figure reports **4.0/5 (~5,918 reviews)**; the broader Constant Contact seller page (aggregating multiple product lines, e.g. "Advanced Automation" rated separately at ~4.5/5) shows **~9,168 reviews** (all FACT, third-party G2 pages via WebSearch snippets, retrieved 2026-09-10 — none independently confirmed via a direct live page render, since g2.com returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch in this pass). **This roughly corroborates — but does not exactly match — the disputed range already recorded in `zoho-campaigns.md`/the benchmark (G2 ~4.0–4.1/5, ~5,770–7,261 reviews): the rating band (4.0–4.1) matches, but review counts gathered independently here run somewhat higher at the top end (~7,383–9,168 depending on which G2 listing) — treat both as UNVERIFIED-exact and re-confirm directly on g2.com/products/constant-contact/reviews before external use.**
- **Capterra rating (FACT, via direct WebFetch of capterra.com/p/31898/Constant-Contact/reviews/, retrieved 2026-09-10):** **4.3/5, 2,947 verified reviews.** Sub-ratings: Ease of Use 4.3, Customer Service 4.2. (One WebSearch snippet cited a divergent "2,892 reviews" / "4.5 stars" figure from a secondary source — the directly-fetched Capterra page figure of 4.3/5, 2,947 reviews is treated as higher-confidence here since it came from a direct page fetch rather than a search snippet.)
- **Liked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra + G2 summaries, retrieved 2026-09-10):** Intuitive drag-and-drop editor requiring no technical expertise; simple contact-list management and segmentation; professional templates and reliable deliverability; straightforward campaign scheduling and basic automation; ease of use broadly (cited in 1,000+ G2 reviews per aggregation); phone support available on all paid tiers.
- **Disliked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Pricing escalates significantly as contact database grows, and billing is based on contacts stored rather than emails sent — the single most consistently repeated complaint; limited advanced-automation/personalization sophistication versus specialized platforms; restrictive template customization; outdated UI in need of modernization; billing practices such as phone-only cancellation and auto-renewal issues.
- **Recurring complaints (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Cost/value tension — reviewers frequently combine praise for ease-of-use with frustration at price escalation; features like "resend to non-openers" and advanced reporting gated behind upgrade tiers.
- **Recurring praise (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Reliability, simplicity, customer/phone support quality.
- **Requested features:** NOT OBSERVED in this pass — TODO (needs dedicated review-mining sort by "most recent"/"lowest rating").
- **Why customers switch away (INFERENCE from complaint themes):** Cost growth as lists scale, and desire for more sophisticated automation/personalization than Constant Contact's builder offers.
- **Why customers choose it over competitors (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + INFERENCE):** Ease of use, deliverability reliability, and availability of phone-based customer support — a differentiator less consistently claimed by Mailchimp/ActiveCampaign/Zoho Campaigns in the review themes gathered so far across this benchmark.

## 13. Strengths / Weaknesses
- **Best features (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Ease of use / low learning curve; reliable deliverability; contact-list management and segmentation; phone-based customer support; drag-and-drop editor.
- **Weakest features (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Pricing model tied to total contacts stored (not send volume), causing steep cost escalation as lists grow; limited advanced-automation/personalization sophistication vs. specialists like ActiveCampaign; dated UI; restrictive template customization; some features gated above the entry Lite tier.

## 15. What We Should Learn
- **RECOMMENDATION — adopt:** Phone-based customer support as a differentiator for SMB/nonprofit segments — this is the most distinctive "why customers choose it" theme found across the three competitors researched so far in this category (derived from §5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK; not yet confirmed whether Mailchimp/ActiveCampaign/Zoho Campaigns offer equivalent phone support, which is TODO).
- **RECOMMENDATION — avoid:** Contacts-stored-based pricing (rather than usage/send-based pricing) as the sole billing axis — this is Constant Contact's most consistent complaint theme and a direct cautionary data point for any pricing-model decision (derived from §2/§5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK).
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate before adopting:** Whether Constant Contact's reported reliability/deliverability edge (no complaint theme surfaced here, vs. Zoho Campaigns' recurring deliverability complaints) reflects a genuine technical difference (e.g., dedicated IP practices, sender-reputation management) or simply a gap in review-mining coverage — needs a matched, deliberate review-mining pass before treating it as a confirmed relative strength (derived from §9, INFERENCE).

## 1. Identity
- **Company (FACT, Wikipedia — "Gusto, Inc.", retrieved 2026-09-10):** Gusto, Inc., founded November 2011 in San Francisco, CA as "ZenPayroll" by Joshua Reeves, Edward Kim, and Tomer London; rebranded to Gusto in September 2015 as it expanded beyond payroll into benefits and HR. Headquartered at 525 20th Street, San Francisco.
- **Category:** HR & Recruiting — payroll-led HR/benefits platform (INFERENCE from product description and pricing structure, retrieved 2026-09-10), narrower in scope than Rippling's HR+IT+Finance breadth but broader than a pure payroll processor.
- **Problem solved (INFERENCE from vendor/aggregator descriptions, retrieved 2026-09-10):** Combines payroll processing (with automated federal/state/local tax filing), benefits administration (health/dental/vision, 401(k), workers' comp via third-party providers), and core HR (onboarding, employee self-service) into one platform, targeted primarily at US-based small businesses.
- **Target users / industries (INFERENCE from pricing/positioning, retrieved 2026-09-10):** Small to mid-sized US businesses; vendor reported to serve "more than 100,000 businesses" (Wikipedia, sourced to company reporting — **UNVERIFIED against an official Gusto press/about page in this pass**).
- **Segment:** SMB primary (INFERENCE — flat/simple per-tier + per-employee pricing structure with no enterprise custom-quote tier surfaced in this pass, unlike Zoho People/BambooHR/Rippling which all offer an enterprise or custom-quote path).
- **Platforms:** Web; mobile app referenced in customer-review themes (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra, retrieved 2026-09-10) — NOT OBSERVED directly (iOS/Android availability not independently confirmed against an official source in this pass).
- **Geographic scope (INFERENCE):** US-only — no international/multi-country payroll support surfaced in pricing or feature descriptions gathered in this pass; one review-summary explicitly names "international operations support" as a gap (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, see Section 5). Contrast with Zoho People (165+ countries claimed) and Rippling (global HR+IT positioning).

## 5. Customer Reviews
- **Source(s):**
  - **G2 (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, corroborated across multiple independent search-result snippets rather than a single direct page fetch — g2.com/products/gusto/reviews returned HTTP 403 to automated WebFetch on 2026-09-10):** Rating consistently reported at **4.6/5**; review count reported inconsistently across sources gathered in this same pass — **8,335** (one search-summary) vs. **8,504** (a second search-summary, matching the figure already on record in zoho-people.md from an earlier pass). **UNVERIFIED — needs a direct browser check of the live G2 page**; treat the review count as approximately 8,300–8,500 pending confirmation, not a precise figure.
  - **Capterra (FACT, capterra.com/p/131882/Gusto/reviews/, retrieved 2026-09-10 via direct fetch):** 4.6/5, based on 4,224 verified user reviews. (A separate search-summary cited 4,157 — within normal review-count drift given ongoing new reviews; the 4,224 figure is the one directly fetched from the live page and should be treated as the more current figure.)
  - **Trustpilot (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, search-summary, retrieved 2026-09-10):** Reported materially lower — 2.4–2.5/5 across ~2,364 reviews in one summary. **UNVERIFIED against a direct Trustpilot fetch**, but the gap between G2/Capterra (4.6) and Trustpilot (~2.4) is itself a notable pattern worth flagging: Trustpilot skews toward negative-experience self-selection industry-wide, but a gap this large (4.6 vs 2.4) is larger than typical and may indicate the G2/Capterra reviewer base (often incentivized/vendor-prompted) diverges meaningfully from the Trustpilot base (typically unprompted, often support-escalation-driven).
- **Liked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra direct fetch + G2 search-summary, retrieved 2026-09-10):** Ease of use / clean, intuitive interface requiring minimal training; affordability and value for small businesses; all-in-one consolidation of payroll, benefits, HR, and tax filing; employee self-service via mobile/desktop; automated payroll runs reported to often take under 10 minutes; responsive customer support "particularly for smaller companies."
- **Disliked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra direct fetch + G2/Trustpilot search-summary, retrieved 2026-09-10):** Limited scalability — described as lacking robustness for growing/complex organizations needing advanced HR/payroll configuration; customer support gaps, particularly slow response times during peak periods and limited after-hours availability; integration challenges with certain third-party systems/timekeeping platforms; inflexible workflows (user roles not customizable); tax-filing error complaints (incorrect W-2s cited as a common trigger) surfaced prominently in the lower-rated review population (Trustpilot-weighted); one specific recurring bug theme: rehired employees' settings are not reset, causing ongoing city/local tax handling issues.
- **Recurring complaints:** Support responsiveness and quality once something goes wrong (a "things work fine until they break, then support is a black hole" pattern per one search-summary characterization); tax-filing accuracy edge cases; inflexibility for organizations that outgrow the SMB-simple model.
- **Recurring praise:** Ease of use, all-in-one payroll+benefits+HR consolidation, pricing/value, automation of tax compliance.
- **Requested features:** TODO — not surfaced in this pass; needs a dedicated review-mining pass sorted by "most recent"/"lowest rating."
- **Why customers switch away (INFERENCE, derived from the disliked-most themes above):** Plausibly organizations that outgrow SMB-scale needs (custom roles, complex multi-entity/international payroll) — consistent with the explicit "limited scalability" and "international operations" gaps noted above, but not yet backed by direct switch-away quotes.
- **Why customers choose it over competitors (INFERENCE):** Simplicity and automated US tax compliance for straightforward small-business payroll, at a lower support/onboarding overhead than platforms with broader scope (Rippling) — not yet backed by direct comparative review quotes.

## 15. What We Should Learn
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate:** Gusto's "payroll runs often take under 10 minutes" praise theme (Section 5 CUSTOMER FEEDBACK) suggests speed/simplicity of the core payroll-run workflow is a high-leverage UX pattern worth studying directly (would require live product access — currently NOT OBSERVED).

- **Problem solved (INFERENCE from vendor/comparison material):** Invoicing, billing/payments, expense tracking, time tracking, and financial reporting, positioned around simplicity for service-based businesses rather than broad ERP-style accounting (per freshbooks.com/pricing summary and fitsmallbusiness.com/nerdwallet comparison summaries, retrieved 2026-09-10).

## 2. Market & Business

### Pricing (FACT — official freshbooks.com/pricing, cross-checked via search-result summary, retrieved 2026-09-10)
| Plan | Monthly price | Billable clients | Users included | Key notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | $23/mo | 5 clients | 1 user | Invoicing, expense tracking, time tracking, estimates |
| Plus | $43/mo | 50 clients | 1 user | Recurring billing, client retainers, sales-tax-time reports, accountant access |
| Premium | $70/mo | Unlimited clients | 1 user + 2 additional team member accounts included | Dedicated account manager, custom integrations, bulk data import |
| Select | Custom pricing | Unlimited | Custom | Dedicated account management, "unlimited everything" |

- **Annual discount (FACT):** 10% off with annual billing (per search-result summary of freshbooks.com/pricing, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Additional users (FACT):** Every plan is effectively single-user by default; additional team members cost $11/mo each, except where included on Premium (per agiled.app/tech.co pricing summaries cross-referencing freshbooks.com, retrieved 2026-09-10 — third-party sourced, recommend confirming directly against freshbooks.com/pricing before external use).
- **Add-ons (FACT, third-party sourced):** Payroll add-on (powered by Gusto, US only) at $40/mo + $6/user; Advanced Payments add-on at $20/mo (not available on Select) (agiled.app summary, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Payment processing fees (FACT, third-party sourced):** 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction for Visa/Mastercard, 3.5% + $0.30 for Amex, 1% for ACH, plus 1.5% surcharge for international or instant payouts (agiled.app summary, retrieved 2026-09-10) — recommend confirming against freshbooks.com official terms before external use.
- **Free plan/trial (FACT):** No free tier; 30-day free trial, no credit card required (multiple sources, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Recent pricing trend (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + third-party sourced):** FreshBooks raised list prices twice in roughly the past year (from ~$19/$33/$60 in early 2025 to $23/$43/$70 as of this pass) per agiled.app, retrieved 2026-09-10; this aligns with a G2-reported customer complaint theme of "yearly rate increases" (see Section 5).
- **Market positioning (CUSTOMER-FEEDBACK/aggregator-sourced):** Positioned as the simpler, more polished invoicing-first tool for freelancers/service businesses — "if you need simple invoicing software with a polished UX, FreshBooks offers a cleaner starting point" — vs. Zoho Books being positioned as the better fit for growing businesses needing scale (fitsmallbusiness.com "FreshBooks vs Zoho Books," retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Key differentiators claimed by vendor/third parties:** Ease of use (G2 sub-score 9.2 per category comparison — see Section 5), strong customer support reputation, project-based invoicing/time-tracking workflow geared to service businesses, payroll add-on (US, via Gusto).

  - Ease of use — "FreshBooks excels in ease of use, scoring 9.2" in a G2 category comparison; frequently cited as simplifying accounting tasks for small business owners.

- **Disliked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, G2 pros/cons summary, retrieved 2026-09-10):**

## 1. Identity
- **Company (FACT):** monday.com Ltd. Originally built as an internal tool at Wix.com (named "Dapulse"), spun off as a separate startup in 2012, commercially launched 2014, renamed to monday.com in 2017, went public (NASDAQ: MNDY) June 10, 2021. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monday.com, monday.com/p/about — retrieved 2026-09-10, via WebSearch summary)
- **Category:** Project & Work Management (marketed by the vendor as a broader "Work OS" spanning Work Management, CRM, Service, and Dev products).
- **Problem solved (FACT, vendor-stated):** A visual, highly customizable "Work OS" for planning, tracking, and automating work — boards/items, multiple views (Timeline, Gantt, Calendar), workflow automations, and AI credits bundled into paid plans. (monday.com/pricing, retrieved 2026-09-10 via WebFetch)
- **Target users / industries (FACT + INFERENCE):** Vendor states it serves "more than 127,000 customers worldwide" across small businesses and enterprises, marketed as a horizontal "Work OS" rather than a single-department tool (helloleads.io / monday.com blog summary, retrieved 2026-09-10 via WebSearch — third-party sourced, flag for re-verification against monday.com's own investor/about material). Separately reported: "over 250,000 customers and more than 60% of Fortune 500 companies" per a different search-result summary citing G2/vendor material — **discrepancy between the two customer-count figures noted, both third-party sourced, needs reconciliation against monday.com's own official stats page before use.**
- **Segment:** Multiple — SMB through enterprise (paid plans require a 3-seat minimum, free plan capped at 2 seats; Enterprise tier with custom pricing and 24/7 support/SLA exists) (FACT, monday.com/pricing, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Platforms (FACT):** Web, mobile apps — "mobile apps" listed on the Free plan (monday.com/pricing, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Ecosystem / sister products it integrates with (FACT):** monday.com is now a suite — separate but related products for CRM, Service, and Dev, each with their own pricing tiers, plus native integrations and AI credits bundled per plan (monday.com/pricing, retrieved 2026-09-10). ## 5. Customer Reviews
- **G2 (FACT, WebSearch-verified via multiple independent search results, retrieved 2026-09-10 — direct g2.com fetch returned HTTP 403, so this is aggregated from search-result summaries of the G2 page rather than a raw page fetch):** monday Work Management (the Work Management product specifically, i.e. the entry most comparable to Zoho Projects/Asana/ClickUp) rates **4.7/5 across 14,955 reviews**. A broader "monday.com Products" umbrella listing (spanning all of monday's product lines — Work Management, CRM, Service, Dev) shows 4.7/5 across 18,961 reviews. **Use the 14,955-review, Work-Management-specific figure for category comparison; the 18,961 figure spans multiple products and is not apples-to-apples with single-product competitors like Asana/ClickUp/Zoho Projects.**
- **Capterra (FACT, WebSearch-verified, retrieved 2026-09-10 — direct fetch not attempted; sourced from search-result summaries of capterra.com):** Figures were inconsistent across summaries — one states "4.7/5 across 460 reviews," another states "4.6 rating with 5,720 verified user reviews" for the same product page (capterra.com/p/147657/monday-com/reviews/). **This discrepancy is flagged as UNVERIFIED — needs a direct, authenticated fetch of the Capterra page to resolve; do not treat either figure as final without re-verification.**
- **Liked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, G2-sourced themes via WebSearch summary, retrieved 2026-09-10):** High customizability/flexible workflow building; intuitive, easy-to-use UI even for non-technical users; strong integration with tools teams already use; automation of routine tasks improving efficiency; specific standout categories per G2: task creation/assignment (94% positive), due dates (94%), task prioritization (93%).
- **Disliked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, G2-sourced themes via WebSearch summary, retrieved 2026-09-10):** Pricing is cited as a main complaint (compounded by the 3-seat minimum on paid plans, which is unfriendly to solo users/very small teams per a Capterra-sourced theme); permissions/security gaps reported (e.g., lack of restriction on who can delete contacts/items in some contexts); no single centralized admin configuration screen, complicating multi-workspace administration; desire for more native integrations vs. reliance on Zapier; slow loading times reported in some reviews; manual cleanup needed after mass updates.
- **Recurring complaints (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Pricing/seat-minimum friction for small teams; admin/permissions gaps; integration library needing refinement; occasional performance/loading-time complaints.
- **Recurring praise (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK):** Customization and visual flexibility; ease of use/onboarding for non-technical users; automation depth; support quality (G2 support rating reported at 4.5/5, praised for responsiveness and knowledge-base comprehensiveness).

## 15. What We Should Learn
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate:** Bundling AI credits directly into every paid tier (rather than gating AI behind a separate premium add-on, as some competitors do) is a differentiator worth comparing against Zoho Projects' Zia AI gating (Zia is excluded from Zoho's Free plan entirely — see `../../01-Zoho-Primary-Products/zoho-projects.md` §10). Derived from Section 2 FACT (monday.com/pricing) — worth a deeper pricing-model comparison once more competitor AI-gating data exists.
- **RECOMMENDATION — avoid:** A hard 3-seat minimum on all paid plans is a recurring CUSTOMER FEEDBACK complaint point (Section 5) — worth flagging as a friction pattern to avoid if targeting solo/very-small-team segments.
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate before adopting:** No single centralized admin configuration screen (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Section 5) suggests multi-workspace/multi-product admin UX is an area where monday.com's "Work OS" breadth (multiple product lines) may be creating administrative complexity — worth watching as we look at how Zoho's own multi-product ecosystem (Projects + CRM + other apps) handles the same challenge.

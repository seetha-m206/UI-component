## 1. Identity
- **Company (FACT):** Titan, founded in 2018 (initially launched as "Flockmail") by Bhavin Turakhia and the founding leadership team behind Directi and Media.net (titan.email/about/, retrieved 2026-09-10; corroborated by inc42.com and cxotoday.com coverage, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Category:** Communication (business/custom-domain email hosting for SMBs).
- **Problem solved (FACT, vendor-stated):** Productivity-focused, professional custom-domain business email built specifically for small businesses, entrepreneurs, and professionals — positioned around "well-designed, cost-effective tools" rather than complex enterprise suites (titan.email/about/, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Target users / industries (FACT, vendor-stated):** Small businesses, entrepreneurs, professionals/creators, and domain registrants acquired through distribution partners (titan.email/about/, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Segment:** SMB / individual professional — Titan explicitly does not target large enterprise the way Google Workspace/Microsoft 365 do (INFERENCE from vendor positioning language, titan.email/about/, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Platforms:** Web, plus mobile app referenced in third-party comparison content (capterra.com pricing summary, retrieved 2026-09-10 — "lightweight mobile app" mentioned; not independently confirmed against an official Titan platform-support page in this pass, flagged UNVERIFIED).
- **Distribution model (FACT — notable structural difference from Zoho Mail):** Titan does not sell mailboxes directly to end users. It is distributed exclusively through partner resellers — WordPress.com, Hostinger, Name.com, HostGator, NameSilo, Rumahweb, and others — via titan.email/signup (search-derived summary of titan.email, retrieved 2026-09-10; corroborated by trustradius.com pricing page, retrieved 2026-09-10).

## 2. Market & Business
- **Founded / product age (FACT):** 2018 (titan.email/about/, retrieved 2026-09-10).

## 5. Customer Reviews
- **Source — G2 (FACT, with caveat):** 4.6/5, reported across multiple search-derived summaries as either 558 or 561 reviews (slight variance between sources, retrieved 2026-09-10). **Direct fetch of g2.com/products/titan-titan/reviews returned HTTP 403 in this pass — the rating/count is relayed via search snippets, not independently loaded from the primary page. Treat as FACT-with-verification-gap per evidence-guidelines.md rule 3.**
- **Source — Capterra (FACT):** 4.5/5, 128 verified reviews (capterra.com/p/195493/Titan-Email/reviews/, retrieved 2026-09-10, directly fetched). Sub-scores: Ease of Use 4.6/5, Customer Service 4.5/5, Features 4.3/5; ~75% likelihood-to-recommend (same source).
- **Historical note (FACT):** Titan was ranked the highest-rated email software on G2 in G2's Summer 2022 report, including #1 in Customer Service for Email (titan.email press release + thedomains.com/businesswire.com coverage, retrieved 2026-09-10); the rating has since settled to 4.6/5 as the review base grew — a normal pattern, not necessarily a quality decline (INFERENCE).
- **Liked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra-sourced):** Clean, intuitive interface; ease of use for beginners and small businesses; read-receipt/tracking feature valued for follow-ups and workflow management; multiple categorizable signatures; strong value for the price ("Perfect for the price point and what I use the email in my freelance business" — direct reviewer quote via Capterra pricing-page summary, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Disliked most (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra-sourced):** Missing/disappearing emails reported in some accounts; slow DNS propagation during setup; inbox-threading behavior some users find inefficient; color-rendering issues in certain email clients; frustration over recent feature monetization — one long-time user noted features "were previously included" but are "now charging for tools" (direct reviewer paraphrase via Capterra, retrieved 2026-09-10).
- **Recurring complaints:** Reliability concerns (missing emails, DNS propagation delays), pricing-structure confusion / recent monetization of previously-free features (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra).
- **Recurring praise:** Affordability, ease of use, read-receipt/scheduling features, customer support quality (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, G2 + Capterra summaries).
- **Requested features:** NOT OBSERVED in this pass — needs a dedicated review-mining pass.
- **Why customers switch away / choose it over competitors:** INFERENCE only — "choose it" plausibly correlates with cost-sensitivity and a preference for a simpler, faster SMB-focused tool over a full office suite; "switch away" plausibly correlates with the reliability complaints (missing emails) or the reseller-only distribution model requiring a domain-partner purchase rather than a direct signup. Needs direct review quotes to upgrade further.

## 15. What We Should Learn
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate before adopting:** Read-receipt / send-later / email-template features are repeatedly cited as Titan's most-loved differentiators (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Section 5) — worth checking whether Zoho Mail already offers equivalents and, if not, whether they're worth prioritizing.
- **RECOMMENDATION — avoid:** Introducing reliability regressions (missing/disappearing mail) and quietly moving previously-free features behind a paywall — both are recurring complaint themes that appear to actively erode trust (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Section 5).
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate:** Titan's reseller-only distribution model (no direct signup, only via domain-registrar/hosting partners) is structurally different from Zoho Mail's direct-signup model — worth a dedicated INFERENCE/analysis pass on whether that distribution strategy affects customer acquisition cost, brand control, or the "switch away" reasons noted in Section 5.
- **RECOMMENDATION — investigate:** Resolve the conflicting pricing figures ($2.00–$2.50/user/mo per one source vs. $1.49/user/mo per Capterra, and conflicting free-trial claims) by fetching an official titan.email pricing page or a specific reseller page directly before using any of these numbers externally (derived from Section 2 caveats).

## 3. What Each Competitor Does Better Than the Others
- **Zoho Campaigns:** Multi-channel (email+SMS+WhatsApp) automation in a single workflow canvas; deep native Zoho CRM/Commerce integration; price-to-value for SMBs (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + FACT feature set).
- **Mailchimp:** Brand recognition / mass-market familiarity, largest review base gathered so far (G2 ~18,416, Capterra ~17,565); broadest integration count gathered so far (300+ native integrations) and the most feature-rich vendor-stated AI push in the category (Intuit Assist + Analytics AI, two distinct 2026 launches) (mailchimp.md §1/§3/§10). Ease of use for non-technical users is its most consistent CUSTOMER FEEDBACK praise theme.
- **ActiveCampaign:** Automation sophistication for experienced marketers/e-commerce, per repeated aggregator positioning — not yet independently verified via direct feature audit.
- **Constant Contact:** Ease of use and phone-based customer support — independently verified via CUSTOMER FEEDBACK (G2/Capterra review-theme synthesis, retrieved 2026-09-10; see `../02-Competitor-Products/marketing-automation/constant-contact.md` §5), not just the aggregator "best overall alternative" framing. Also shows the only recurring *reliability/deliverability praise* theme (rather than complaint) gathered so far in this category — tentatively a relative strength versus Zoho Campaigns' deliverability complaints, though not a controlled comparison. Its clearest weakness, symmetrically, is contacts-stored-based pricing that reviewers say escalates painfully with list growth.
- **Brevo, GetResponse, MailerLite, Campaign Monitor, Moosend, HubSpot Marketing Hub:** TODO.

## 4. Patterns to Adopt
- Single automation canvas spanning multiple channels (email/SMS/WhatsApp) rather than channel-siloed tools (Zoho Campaigns) — CUSTOMER FEEDBACK and vendor FACT support this as a genuine SMB-relevant differentiator, though not yet benchmarked against how deeply Mailchimp/ActiveCampaign integrate SMS.
- Native CRM integration as a stickiness/switching-cost lever — recurring top "liked most" theme for Zoho Campaigns.

## 5. Patterns to Avoid
- Under-investing in automation-builder sophistication relative to specialist competitors once past basic SMB use cases — Zoho Campaigns' most consistent "growth ceiling" complaint, apparently contrasted against ActiveCampaign's stronger automation reputation (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + aggregator positioning, not yet independently feature-audited).
- Inconsistent email deliverability — Zoho Campaigns' most serious recurring complaint; root cause not yet investigated (shared IP pools vs. authentication setup vs. onboarding gaps all remain TODO).
- Billing tied purely to contacts stored rather than send volume/activity — Constant Contact's most consistent complaint theme, with reviewers describing steep bill escalation for large but infrequently-emailed lists (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `../02-Competitor-Products/marketing-automation/constant-contact.md` §2/§5).

## 6. Customer Pain Points Across the Market
- Deliverability inconsistency — confirmed for **both** Zoho Campaigns (bounces, unexplained send failures) and Mailchimp (emails routed to spam) via independently gathered CUSTOMER FEEDBACK (zoho-campaigns.md §5, mailchimp.md §5/§9). Constant Contact shows no equivalent deliverability complaint theme in this pass (see `../02-Competitor-Products/marketing-automation/constant-contact.md` §9) and instead shows a deliverability *praise* theme — this now looks like a genuine two-of-three-researched-products pattern rather than a single-vendor issue, though ActiveCampaign still needs the same review-mining pass before fully generalizing (**TODO**).
- Pricing tied to contacts stored rather than usage/value — confirmed as Constant Contact's top complaint theme; Mailchimp's top complaint theme is closely related (pricing escalates with contact-list size, plus a reduced free tier and automation moved behind a paid tier in 2025-2026 — mailchimp.md §2/§5), though Mailchimp's billing is usage-tiered rather than pure contacts-stored like Constant Contact's, so the mechanisms differ even though the customer-facing complaint ("it gets expensive as my list grows") rhymes. Whether ActiveCampaign/Zoho Campaigns share this exact complaint is still **TODO** for ActiveCampaign (Zoho Campaigns shows the opposite — a price/value praise theme).
- Automation-builder sophistication ceiling — confirmed for **both** Zoho Campaigns ("adequate for simple sequences" per its own reviewers) and Mailchimp ("pretty basic unless you pay for premium features" per its reviewers), each contrasted against ActiveCampaign's stronger aggregator-cited automation-sophistication positioning (zoho-campaigns.md §5, mailchimp.md §5/§13). This is now a two-product-confirmed pattern suggesting mass-market/SMB-priced tools trade automation depth for accessibility, with ActiveCampaign occupying the "goes deeper" niche — still not independently feature-audited head-to-head.
- Support quality gated by pricing tier — new theme from Mailchimp (support described as weak below the Premium/$350mo tier, mailchimp.md §5) — not yet checked against Zoho Campaigns/ActiveCampaign; Constant Contact by contrast offers phone support more broadly per its own record, making this a potential Constant Contact differentiator (**TODO** to confirm directly).

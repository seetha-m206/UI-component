## 3. What Each Competitor Does Better Than the Others
- **Zoho Projects:** Depth of "enterprise-grade" PM capability (Gantt, dependencies, resource/portfolio views, critical path/baseline analysis) bundled into low-priced SMB tiers (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + official pricing/feature page).
- **Asana:** Ease of use for cross-functional/creative-team collaboration and goals-to-work traceability (Portfolios/Goals at Advanced+); broadest native-integration directory documented in this benchmark (200+, per `asana.md` §1/§3); AI Studio bundled from the Starter tier rather than reserved for a top-only tier. Now a fully-researched record — no longer aggregator-summary-only.
- **ClickUp:** Free-tier feature breadth (unlimited tasks/members, multiple views, chat/whiteboards at no cost) — a repeatedly cited value driver in gathered review summaries.
- **monday.com:** Visual customizability and ease of building tailored workflows, plus AI credits bundled into every paid tier rather than gated behind a premium add-on (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + official pricing page, `monday-com.md` §2/§5). Highest raw G2 review count gathered in this category so far (14,955, Work Management-specific).
- **Jira, Microsoft Project:** TODO — insufficient data gathered in this pass.

## 4. Patterns to Adopt
- Bundling genuinely deep PM capability into affordable/free tiers rather than gating it heavily behind enterprise pricing — validated as a satisfaction driver for both Zoho Projects ("enterprise features at SMB prices") and ClickUp (generous free tier), i.e. a pattern that recurs across at least two products in this category, not just one (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, both records).

## 5. Patterns to Avoid
- Shipping a mobile app materially behind desktop feature parity — confirmed as a Zoho Projects complaint theme (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK) and consistent with the same pattern already documented for Zoho Social in the sister benchmark (`social-media-management.md`); not yet confirmed for Asana/ClickUp specifically (their mobile experience is TODO), so this is flagged as a Zoho-specific risk to avoid rather than a category-wide norm.
- Feature breadth without apparent progressive disclosure appears to produce a "learning curve" complaint regardless of pricing model — both Zoho Projects (paid-tier depth) and ClickUp (free-tier breadth) independently draw this same complaint theme (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, both records) — suggests the risk is in UI/onboarding design, not in feature count itself, but this remains an INFERENCE pending direct UI observation of either product.

## 6. Customer Pain Points Across the Market
- Learning curve / onboarding complexity — documented for Zoho Projects, Asana, and ClickUp independently in this pass (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, all three records) — the most consistent cross-product pain point found so far in this category. Asana's case is now fully documented and notable because it coexists with an explicit "ease of use" market position (`asana.md` §5/§15) — an internal tension not seen as starkly in the other records. monday.com's own complaint theme leans more toward pricing/seat-minimums and admin/permissions gaps than "learning curve" per se, so this pattern is not (yet) confirmed for monday.com specifically.
- Mobile app parity — documented for Zoho Projects only; still TODO for Asana (Section 11 marked NOT OBSERVED in `asana.md`, only an incidental mobile-security-controls fact surfaced) and for ClickUp/monday.com/Jira before this can be generalized as category-wide.
- Pricing/seat-minimum friction — documented for monday.com (3-seat minimum on all paid plans, pricing cited as a main G2 complaint) and now also for Asana, though the shape differs: Asana has no seat minimum but its Advanced tier (~$25/user/mo) is cited as a switch-away trigger at scale (~$2,499/mo for 100 users, per `asana.md` §5), and its Enterprise pricing is entirely unpublished ("contact sales"). Not yet checked against ClickUp/Zoho Projects at equivalent scale, so still not fully generalized as category-wide, but now confirmed independently in two of four scored products.
- Feature-gating behind higher tiers — Asana's native time tracking (Advanced-tier-only) is documented as both a requested feature and a switch-away driver (`asana.md` §5) — a specific instance of the broader "pay more to unlock expected functionality" pattern also seen in Zoho Projects' AI/advanced-feature tier-gating (Section 4/10 of `zoho-projects.md`).

## 3. What Each Competitor Does Better Than the Others
- **Zoho Analytics:** Lower cost, permanent free tier, native Zoho-ecosystem integration, "Ask Zia" natural-language query layer (FACT/CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Section 2/5/10 of `../01-Zoho-Primary-Products/zoho-analytics.md`).
- **Tableau:** Visualization depth/interactivity, breadth of data-source connectivity (SQL, Snowflake, Excel, Salesforce), and proactive AI-driven insight delivery via Tableau Pulse/Explain Data/Tableau Einstein (Agentforce-integrated) — FACT/CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `../02-Competitor-Products/bi-analytics/tableau.md` Sections 3/5/10. Weakest areas: steep learning curve for advanced authoring (calculated fields, LOD expressions), high per-seat cost at the Creator tier (up to $75–$115/user/month), and reported performance degradation on large datasets (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `tableau.md` Section 5).
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Microsoft 365/Azure integration and reported beginner-friendliness relative to Tableau (per third-party comparison summaries).
- **Qlik Sense:** Associative-analytics-engine exploration model (vendor-differentiated against "query-based" competitors), strong Capterra scores for dashboard interactivity and self-service ease of use (4.5/5, 262 reviews, direct fetch), and a capacity(GB)-based pricing metric that decouples cost from user count above its entry tier (FACT, official pricing page, `qlik-sense.md` Section 2). Weakest area: total cost of ownership once connectors/implementation/support add-ons are included, and vendor support quality (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, Capterra).
- **Google Looker, Sisense, Domo:** TODO — insufficient data gathered in this pass to characterize differentiation with confidence.

## 4. Patterns to Adopt
- Permanent free tier with a reasonably usable row/workspace allowance (Zoho Analytics: 10,000 rows / 5 workspaces) as a low-friction acquisition funnel — derived from Section 2 FACT of the anchor product record.
- Natural-language query layer that returns a chart + numbers + interpretation in a single response, rather than requiring the user to build a dashboard first — derived from Zoho Analytics' "Ask Zia" FACT description. Tableau independently validates the category-level value of this pattern with its own comparably deep AI stack (Tableau Pulse: proactive NL Q&A + anomaly alerts; Explain Data: automated statistical driver analysis) — FACT, `tableau.md` Section 10 — though neither product's AI sentiment has been independently verified via customer reviews yet, so this remains a candidate pattern pending a comparative AI-features + sentiment pass.
- Proactive/push-based insight delivery (Tableau Pulse delivering anomaly alerts and NL-answerable insights directly into Slack/email, rather than requiring the user to open a dashboard) — a pattern not present in Zoho Analytics' current "Ask Zia" (which appears request-driven) — derived from `tableau.md` Section 10 FACT. Worth evaluating as a differentiator.

## 5. Patterns to Avoid
- Under-investing in large-dataset and multi-source/complex-join performance if the product needs to serve past self-service SMB reporting — this is Zoho Analytics' most consistent complaint theme (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, G2 + Capterra), and Tableau reviewers separately report a related "slow with large datasets" theme (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `tableau.md` Section 5/9) — suggestive that large-dataset performance is a category-wide tradeoff rather than Zoho-specific, though the two products' underlying failure modes differ and a direct comparative study has not been done. Confirming this against Power BI/Qlik/Looker is **TODO**.
- Requiring deep technical/analyst-level skill (calculated fields, LOD expressions, table calculations) for advanced analysis without a strong natural-language on-ramp — this is Tableau's most consistent complaint theme (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `tableau.md` Section 5) and a cautionary pattern given Tableau is otherwise praised for visualization depth — depth and accessibility need not be mutually exclusive.
- High per-seat/role-based pricing without a permanent low-cost entry tier — Tableau reviewers explicitly cite per-seat cost (Creator $75–$115/user/month) and price increases as reasons for switching away (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `tableau.md` Section 5), a concrete negative data point Zoho Analytics' free-permanent-tier + per-account model avoids.

## 6. Customer Pain Points Across the Market
- Advanced-analysis / large-dataset / complex-join limitations — confirmed for Zoho Analytics (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK). A related but distinct signal now exists for Tableau: reviewers separately cite "slow performance with large datasets" as a recurring complaint (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `tableau.md` Section 5/9) — so large-dataset performance concerns appear on *both* researched products so far, though the underlying cause may differ (Zoho: join/dataset-size ceilings; Tableau: general large-dataset slowness). Not yet checked against Power BI/Qlik/Looker review themes — **TODO**.
- Learning curve — reported for Zoho Analytics by some users despite "ease of use" being a simultaneous praise theme (likely correlates with use-case complexity, per INFERENCE in the anchor product record). Tableau shows a stronger, more consistent version of this same theme: a "steep learning curve" for advanced features (calculated fields, LOD expressions, table calculations) is one of its most recurring complaints, with reviewers noting it "is not for early-stage data professionals" (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `tableau.md` Section 5). This suggests learning curve may be a category-wide tradeoff for feature depth, not a Zoho-specific issue — strengthens but does not yet fully confirm this pattern; Power BI/Qlik/Looker comparison still **TODO**.
- Pricing/cost at scale — new theme from Tableau research: per-seat licensing cost (Creator ~$75–$115/user/month) and reported price increases are explicitly cited by reviewers as a reason for switching away from Tableau (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `tableau.md` Section 5). Not yet checked whether this is a category-wide enterprise-BI pain point or Tableau-specific — **TODO**.

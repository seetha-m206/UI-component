## 3. What Each Competitor Does Better Than the Others
- **Zoho Social:** Ecosystem bundling with CRM/Desk; unified compose+monitor dashboard (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK).
- **Sprout Social:** Built-in CRM-style contact profiles from social interactions (third-party positioning claim, not yet independently verified); #1-ranked social listening depth (FACT, G2 Winter/Spring 2026 reports); Smart Inbox consolidation and strong onboarding support (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK); newly-GA agentic AI layer (Trellis) spanning publish/listen/inbox/reporting (FACT, vendor press release, July 2026 GA).
- **Hootsuite:** Breadth/maturity of analytics and competitive benchmarking (per search-result summary of vendor material — needs independent verification).
- **Agorapulse:** Price-to-value relative to Sprout Social/Hootsuite (Capterra value-for-money sub-score advantage, CUSTOMER FEEDBACK); unified inbox + social-CRM contact history; agency-specific collaboration tooling (client/team assignment) called out explicitly in vendor positioning and corroborated by review themes praising centralized management (see `../02-Competitor-Products/social-media-management/agorapulse.md` Section 5).

## 4. Patterns to Adopt
- Unified "publish + monitor + reply" single-screen pattern (Zoho Social's "unified compose+monitor," Sprout Social's "Smart Inbox," and Agorapulse's "unified inbox" praise theme) — now recurring across **three** products, low risk to treat as a category-wide expectation rather than a single-vendor quirk.
- Deep, dedicated social-listening/monitoring capability (Sprout Social — #1-ranked G2 category product) — the single most differentiated praised feature found so far in this benchmark; worth treating as a genuine investment area rather than a checkbox feature (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + FACT, G2 rankings).
- Strong, human-supported onboarding (Sprout Social's "helpful setup calls and responsive live chat" theme) as a way to offset a complex/expensive product's adoption friction (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK).

## 5. Patterns to Avoid
- Shipping a mobile app materially behind desktop parity — confirmed independently in **two** products now: Zoho Social (most consistent complaint) and Sprout Social (crashes, long load times, Instagram caption bugs, mobile-to-desktop sync gaps). Strong enough signal to treat as a category-wide risk, not a single-vendor quirk (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, both products).
- Per-user/per-profile pricing that penalizes small teams and solo users — a recurring complaint in Agorapulse's G2/Capterra reviews (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK); worth a pricing-model caution flag if Zoho Social ever considers a similar per-seat structure.
- A pricing ladder where the entry tier's real feature set is narrower than what marketing implies, producing a "surprise upgrade" experience during evaluation — a specific, named complaint theme in Sprout Social's reviews (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK); relevant if Zoho Social ever restructures its own free/Standard tier boundary.
- High cumulative per-seat cost at scale, compounded by paid add-ons and multi-year contracts — cited by ~30% of Sprout Social reviewers as a barrier (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK), and echoed in Agorapulse's lower value-for-money sub-score vs. Zoho Social.

## 6. Customer Pain Points Across the Market
- Mobile parity/reliability gap — confirmed in **two** products now: Zoho Social (weaker feature depth vs. desktop) and Sprout Social (crashes, sync failures, load times). Whether this is category-wide or limited to these two is still **TODO** for Hootsuite/Agorapulse mobile-specific feedback, but the pattern is no longer a single-vendor signal.
- Media/video posting reliability (Agorapulse — confirmed via G2/Capterra: carousel/size limits, video scheduling gaps, upload failures). Whether this is category-wide (third-party platform API constraint) or Agorapulse-specific is **TODO** — needs the same check against Zoho Social/Hootsuite/Sprout Social media-upload feedback.
- Pricing structure friction — both Sprout Social (add-ons/multi-year contracts, "surprise upgrade" from entry tier) and Agorapulse (per-user/per-profile penalizing small teams) show CUSTOMER FEEDBACK-confirmed pricing-model complaints, though the specific complaint shape differs (opaque upsell vs. per-seat penalty). Worth checking whether Hootsuite/Zoho Social show either pattern before generalizing further.

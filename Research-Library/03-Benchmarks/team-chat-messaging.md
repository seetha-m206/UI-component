## 3. What Each Competitor Does Better Than the Others
- **Zoho Cliq:** Lowest cited price point among the three; combines synchronous and asynchronous communication tools in one product; AI-powered chat summarization as a vendor-stated differentiator; deep native integration with the broader Zoho business-app ecosystem (CRM, Projects, Books, Desk, People, Analytics, Creator) (FACT/CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `../01-Zoho-Primary-Products/zoho-cliq.md` Sections 2–5, 10).
- **Slack:** Highest customer-satisfaction ratings and largest review base of the three; largest cited third-party integration marketplace (~2,500+ apps), making it the most vendor-neutral/best-of-breed choice for organizations with a diverse tool stack (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + third-party-sourced FACT, `../02-Competitor-Products/team-chat-messaging/slack.md` Sections 3–5).
- **Microsoft Teams:** Combines chat and full video meetings/calls in one application, positioned as an advantage over a Slack+separate-conferencing-tool setup; near-zero marginal cost for organizations already on a Microsoft 365 subscription, a structural pricing advantage neither Slack nor (to the same degree) Zoho Cliq can offer (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + INFERENCE, `../02-Competitor-Products/team-chat-messaging/microsoft-teams.md` Sections 2–5).
- **Google Chat, Discord, Mattermost:** TODO — insufficient independent data gathered in this pass to characterize their relative strengths.

## 4. Patterns to Adopt
- Combining synchronous (calls, video, screen share) and asynchronous (voice/video messages) communication into a single product, rather than requiring users to switch to a separate conferencing tool — a differentiator explicitly claimed by both Zoho Cliq and (in a narrower form, chat+meetings) Microsoft Teams (FACT/vendor-stated + comparison-sourced, cross-referenced across `zoho-cliq.md` Section 2 and `team-chat-messaging/microsoft-teams.md` Section 2).
- Native, deep integration with a company's own broader product suite as a retention/stickiness strategy (Zoho Cliq ↔ Zoho apps; Microsoft Teams ↔ Microsoft 365 apps) — mirrors the same ecosystem-bundling pattern already documented for Zoho Mail and Microsoft 365/Outlook in the Communication category benchmark (cross-referenced, `communication.md`).
- A large, well-curated third-party app marketplace (Slack's ~2,500+ apps) as an alternative growth lever to suite-bundling, appealing specifically to organizations that do not want to standardize on one vendor's full ecosystem (third-party sourced, `slack.md` Section 3/15).

## 5. Patterns to Avoid
- Voice/video call reliability gaps relative to category expectations — a specific, repeated complaint theme for Zoho Cliq (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `zoho-cliq.md` Section 5), while Slack and Teams are not flagged with the same complaint in sources gathered this pass.
- High resource/RAM usage on end-user machines — a specific, named complaint for Microsoft Teams on lower-spec hardware (comparison-sourced, `team-chat-messaging/microsoft-teams.md` Section 5/9), and worth checking against the similarly-themed "resource-heavy" complaint already logged for the Outlook client in `communication/microsoft-365.md` Section 5 — possibly a broader pattern across Microsoft's client applications, but this is INFERENCE pending dedicated verification.
- Overly restrictive free-tier limits (Slack's reported 90-day message-history cutoff) that reviewers explicitly call a pain point for smaller teams (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK, `slack.md` Section 5) — worth weighing against Zoho Cliq's "forever free" framing, which does not carry the same cited history-limit complaint (though Cliq's free-tier limitations were not independently review-mined to the same depth in this pass).

## 6. Customer Pain Points Across the Market
- Zoho Cliq: connectivity/reliability dependency on a stable internet connection; voice/video calling described as less refined/stable than Slack's or Teams' (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK — G2/Capterra, `zoho-cliq.md` Section 5).
- Slack: free-tier message-history and feature limits called "very restrictive for smaller teams"; per-user costs "add up" as teams grow; search on older content requires precise keywords (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK — G2, `slack.md` Section 5).
- Microsoft Teams: perceived high cost when not already bundled into a Microsoft 365 subscription; additional paid upgrades required for advanced features; resource-heavy on lower-spec hardware (CUSTOMER FEEDBACK + comparison-sourced, `team-chat-messaging/microsoft-teams.md` Section 5).

# Research Library — Competitive Product Research & Benchmarking

## 1. Purpose

Before building the reverse-engineering/component library, the first task is to build a **structured competitive research library**.

The purpose is not simply to collect information about Zoho.

The goal is to create a repeatable research and benchmarking system that answers:

- What products exist?
- What problems do they solve?
- Who uses them?
- What features do they provide?
- Who are their competitors?
- What do customers like and dislike?
- Which product has the best UI/UX?
- Which has the strongest technical/backend implementation?
- Which has the strongest features, workflows, integrations, analytics, AI, etc.?
- What patterns can we learn from the market?
- What should we adopt, improve, or avoid when building our own products?

The research library will later become an input to the **UI/component library and AI knowledge/memory system**.

# 19. Research Evidence

Every important finding should have evidence.

Possible evidence types:

- Official product documentation
- Official pricing pages
- Official feature pages
- Product screenshots
- Product demonstrations
- Customer reviews
- Customer discussions
- Public technical documentation
- Browser/UI observations
- Network observations where permitted
- Performance observations
- Independent reviews
- Market reports where appropriate

Each research record should distinguish:

```text
FACT
↓
Directly observed/documented

INFERENCE
↓
Reasonable technical/business conclusion

OPINION
↓
Analyst judgement

RECOMMENDATION
↓
What we should do based on the evidence
```

This prevents assumptions from being stored as facts.

# 25. Core Principle

The research should answer three levels of questions:

### Level 1 — What exists?

```text
Products
Features
Competitors
Customers
Pricing
```

### Level 2 — How does it work?

```text
UI
Screens
Components
Workflows
Backend
APIs
Technical behavior
```

### Level 3 — What should we learn from it?

```text
Best patterns
Weak patterns
Customer pain points
Market gaps
UX opportunities
Technical opportunities
Product recommendations
```

The final objective is therefore:

> **Research the market → understand the products → benchmark competitors → identify the best patterns → convert the knowledge into a reusable research/component/AI knowledge system.**
